# jp.constitution — the Constitution of Japan (日本国憲法)

The preamble and all 103 articles of the Constitution of Japan (promulgated 3 November 1946, in force
3 May 1947), formalized with deontics: `duty`, `prohibition`, `liberty`, `power`, `immunity`, competing
interpretations, judgment channels and computed thresholds and terms. Package language: comments English,
official labels Japanese (`label ja official` quotes the pinned text), English glosses `unofficial`.

**Status: not a complete executable formalization.** Every dispositive sentence is either an executable rule or
position, or carries a reasoned classification; technical gaps that the language or the evaluators cannot express
are listed below and in `QUESTIONS.md`. They are unfinished work, not declarations.

## Source

e-Gov Law Search API v2, law revision `321CONSTITUTION_19470503_000000000000000` (structured extraction by XML
nodes; `sources/jp-constitution/provenance.json` has the address, the revision and the hashes; the raw response
is kept). One pinned edition, Japanese. No English translation is pinned as a second edition. Fragments
(paragraph by paragraph, 176) are generated by `corpus/tools/sources/sync/sync_jp_constitution_fragments.py`
(`--check`), the bytes by `corpus/tools/sources/fetch/fetch_jp_constitution_text.py`. The unit of the §33.1
measure is `ja_article` (103 of 103).

## What is modelled with care

* Thresholds are computed by multiplication, each with its own denominator: Art. 53 (one quarter of ALL members),
  55/57/58/59(2) (two thirds of those PRESENT), 56 (one third of all for the quorum; a majority of those present;
  the presiding officer on a tie), 96 (two thirds of ALL members of each House, then a majority of votes cast),
  79(3), 95 (votes cast). Exact boundaries and the values next to them are tested; an empty vote never passes.
* Terms: Art. 54 (40 and 30 days), 69 and 70, 100 (six months by `add_calendar_period`), 79(2) (ten years), 91 (a year).
  The terms that EXCLUDE the recess of the Diet (Art. 59(4): 60 days; 60(2): 30 days; 67(2): 10 days) subtract the
  recess: the recess days are facts of the case (`recess_day`) and the counted days are calendar days minus recess
  days (AUDIT.md, item 5, representation B). They are never ordinary calendar terms.
* Art. 100–103 are modelled in the historical domain (windows, computed dates, scenarios with historical decision,
  knowledge and legal time). Their historical character is not a reason to leave them unexecuted.
* Evaluative elements are not arithmetic: judgment relations with the authority named by the text
  (公共の福祉, 残虐な刑罰, 健康で文化的な最低限度の生活, 正当な補償, 公の秩序又は善良の風俗, 誠実に, 直ちに, …) or adjudicated
  facts (`origin adjudicated`); a court's decision is an input, never computed.
* Readings: Art. 9 (`LiteralPacifistReading`, `NecessaryMinimumSelfDefenseReading`), Art. 7(3)/69 (dissolution),
  Art. 66(2) 文民. Claims about what the Government or a court holds are backed by primary sources
  (`docs/foundations/jp-constitution/INTERPRETATION-SOURCES.md`, `coverage/J*-interpretations.md`); what could not be
  confirmed is marked unverified and is not asserted.
* Ordinary statutes («法律の定めるところにより») are explicit dependency contracts (input facts or `external`
  relations); their content is not invented (the Imperial House Law, the Diet Law, the Cabinet Law, the Court
  Organization Law, the election laws, the tax and budget laws, the local autonomy law, the referendum law).

## Boundaries (open work)

The technical gaps, each with a minimal reproduction, are in `QUESTIONS.md`: recess subtraction not expressible in
`.law` (representation B is used); `legal_time` not readable by a rule; a `valid_when` cannot read the effect of another
`power`; the unbounded repetition of the Art. 79(2) review (a ladder of five reviews); a Cabinet incompetent under Art. 71
still gives effective advice for dissolution and designation; the difference between the two evaluators in proof graphs
of NEITHER queries with several `count distinct` aggregates in one rule is qualified as a SPEC gap plus a latent defect
of both implementations (`docs/foundations/jp-constitution/audit/13-proof-graph-orphan-assertions/QUALIFICATION.md`,
errata not yet issued; the package avoids the trigger).
Provisions by nature not executable (declarations of principle: the preamble, Art. 1 the symbol, the motive clause of
Art. 9, «constant endeavour» of Art. 12, Art. 97's account of the struggle, …) are declared with reasons taken from their
text in the coverage map.
